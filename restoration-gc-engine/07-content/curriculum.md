# Education Curriculum Spine

The 30-day evergreen calendar (`30_day_evergreen.md`) and storm-response pack (`storm_response_pack.md`) draw from this curriculum. Every module must pass the Compliance-Gate Agent and carry the state-scoped disclaimer for any state it references — see `09-docs/state_law_reference.md` for the underlying legal citations.

| Module | Core teaching | Owner-type emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| Storm & roof condition | Hail/wind damage signatures; deferred-maintenance vs. storm-damage distinction (the line insurers use to deny); condition scoring; documentation chain (dated photos, date of loss, full policy) | Asset managers, REITs, industrial |
| Policy provisions | ACV vs. RCV and depreciation holdback; matching/uniformity; ordinance-or-law (code-upgrade) coverage; coinsurance penalties | All; coinsurance is acute for churches/synagogues/nonprofits (often 80–90% clauses) |
| Deadlines & process | Proof of loss, notice windows, the appraisal clause, supplemental claims | FL owners (1-year window is the sharpest deadline we teach), all states otherwise |
| State rules | TX Ch. 542/542A prompt-pay & pre-suit notice; FL SB 2-A; OK/IL public-adjuster rules | By geography — never teach one state's rule as if it applies nationally |
| Who does what | Public adjuster vs. contractor vs. attorney roles and legal limits (Stonewater Roofing) | NN-lease owners, first-time claimants |
| Underpayment/denial patterns | Excessive depreciation, short inspections, scope gaps (tear-off, flashing, HVAC curbs), repeated documentation demands used to reset response-time clocks | Portfolio owners, REITs |
| Roof ownership & lease structure | Who is financially responsible for the roof under a given lease (standard NNN vs. absolute-net/bondable) — establish this *before* any other education track applies | NN-lease/dollar-store landlords specifically |

## Format Priority (highest lead-gen intent → broadest awareness)

1. **Interactive tools / lead magnets** (`05-lead-magnets/`): roof-claim readiness scorecard, claim-deadline checker, "is my claim underpaid?" diagnostic, roof-life/ROI calculator, free hail-impact property report.
2. **Document posts** (carousels-as-PDF) and downloadable checklists / storm-response playbooks.
3. **Newsletters** (LinkedIn native + HubSpot email) for compounding audience.
4. **Short video and LinkedIn Live.**
5. **Text posts** for thought leadership; **webinars** for high-intent portfolio owners.

Each lead magnet, carousel, and newsletter section produced from this curriculum should be tagged with the module(s) it draws from so the Compliance-Gate Agent and counsel review can trace any claim back to its underlying legal citation.
